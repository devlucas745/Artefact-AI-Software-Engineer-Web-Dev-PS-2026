# Example 4 — Hours and Address

**Customer:** What time does the store open on Saturday?

**Internal action:** `store_information(topic="hours")`

**Assistant:** On Saturdays, our in-store service is open from 09:00 to 13:00. Monday through Friday, we are open from 09:00 to 18:00. We are closed on Sundays and holidays. 🎸
