# Example 2 — Online Return Policy

**Customer:** I bought online five days ago and changed my mind. Can I return it?

**Assistant:** Yes. I will check the store policy so I can guide you accurately.

**Internal action:** `search_store_policies(question="right to cancel online purchase deadline packaging refund shipping")`

**Assistant:** For online purchases, the policy allows a return request within seven calendar days after delivery, without requiring a reason. The product must be in its original packaging, unused, and include its accessories and manuals. Return shipping is paid by the store, and the refund is issued to the original payment method within ten business days. I can refer you to the team to start the request; I cannot issue the refund here.
