1. ABOUT EMPÓRIO DA MÚSICA

Empório da Música is a musical instrument store founded in 2008 in Campo Grande, Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil. We serve beginner, intermediate, and professional musicians with guitars, basses, acoustic guitars, drums, keyboards, wind instruments, orchestral strings, and ukuleles.

We sell musical instruments exclusively. We do not sell accessories such as strings, picks, cables, cases, pedals, or amplifiers.

Mission: Make quality musical instruments more accessible in Central-West Brazil through specialized service and competitive prices.

3. PAYMENT METHODS

- PIX: payment in full with a 5% discount on the list price.
- Debit card: payment in full; all major brands accepted.
- Credit card: up to 12 interest-free installments; minimum installment of R$ 100.00.
- Bank slip: payment in full; settlement within up to three business days.

Installment rules:
- Up to 3 installments: no interest and no minimum installment, except installments below R$ 50.00.
- 4 to 6 installments: no interest, minimum installment of R$ 80.00.
- 7 to 12 installments: no interest, minimum installment of R$ 100.00.
- Combining payment methods, such as PIX plus card, is allowed for purchases above R$ 2,000.00.

4. EXCHANGES AND RETURNS

The store follows Brazil's Consumer Protection Code (Law 8.078/90).

4.1 Online right of withdrawal:
- The customer may request a return within seven calendar days after delivery, without providing a reason.
- The product must be in its original packaging, unused, and include all accessories and manuals.
- The refund is issued to the original payment method within ten business days.
- Return shipping is paid by the store.

4.2 Defective products:
- Manufacturing defects may be exchanged within 30 calendar days after purchase.
- After 30 days, the customer must contact the manufacturer under its warranty. The store may assist upon request.
- Damage caused by misuse, falls, excessive moisture, or unauthorized modifications is not covered.

4.3 Preference exchanges:
- Exchanges for color, model, or size are allowed within seven days, subject to availability.
- The product must be in perfect condition and in its original packaging.
- Price differences are charged or refunded as applicable.

4.4 Items not eligible for exchange:
- Personalized instruments or custom adjustments.
- Clearance products explicitly marked as final sale.
- Mouthpieces for wind instruments, for hygiene reasons.

5. SHIPPING AND DELIVERY

5.1 Campo Grande metropolitan area:
- Free shipping for orders above R$ 500.00.
- Fixed R$ 35.00 fee for orders below R$ 500.00.
- Estimated delivery: one to three business days.
- Delivery is made by the store's courier. The customer is contacted before delivery.

5.2 Other cities:
The store uses Correios PAC, Correios SEDEX, and Jadlog. Estimated delivery is five to twelve business days for PAC, two to five for SEDEX, and three to eight for Jadlog. Tracking and shipping insurance are included.

Shipping cost is calculated from destination ZIP code, product weight, and dimensions. Large instruments may require an individual quote. If a package arrives damaged, refuse delivery and contact the store immediately.

5.3 Tracking:
The tracking code is sent by email and WhatsApp after dispatch. It normally starts with BR and ends with BR.

6. PROMOTIONS AND DISCOUNTS

Promotions are valid only when marked active in the system. Examples include the store anniversary, Black Friday, back-to-school season, and inventory clearance. PIX discounts cannot be combined with another promotion.

7. CUSTOMER SERVICE

For questions not covered by this manual, the customer should be referred to the human service team. The virtual assistant must not promise cancellations, refunds, changes, or other external actions that it cannot execute.
